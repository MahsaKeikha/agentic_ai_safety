# System: demo-chat-assistant

## Purpose
Internal chat assistant for employees to summarize docs and draft email.

## Users
All staff with company login.

## Data
Users may paste internal documents. No automatic access to production databases.

## Capabilities
- Answer questions from pasted text
- Summarize
- Draft messages

## Explicit non goals
- No tool calling into production systems in v1
- No medical or legal advice mode
